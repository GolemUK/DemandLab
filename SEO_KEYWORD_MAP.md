## Search and indexing checks

- Production origin responds at `https://demandlab.com.ua/`; `https://demanded.com.ua/` did not resolve during this audit.
- `/sitemap.xml` and `/sitemap-index.xml` respond with HTTP 200 and XML. The sitemap contains 34 localized URLs for pages generated from the published site; the index points to `/sitemap.xml`.
- `/robots.txt` responds with HTTP 200, allows crawling, and lists `https://demandlab.com.ua/sitemap.xml`.
- Production article pages render their full text in server-generated HTML with one H1, unique title and description, canonical, and reciprocal `uk`/`en` hreflang. Production previews are marked noindex; 404 is noindex.
- No new article was created: the existing bilingual Google-storage guide already covers device versus cloud storage, Gmail/Drive/Photos, trash, backups, delays and data-loss risks. It was checked against current Google Help and clarified for Photos deletion versus device-space cleanup and the 30-minute/48–72-hour guidance. Source check date is 2026-10-19.
- No search volumes, keyword difficulty, Google positions, or indexing status are asserted. The search-results sample is qualitative and time-bound.
