## How to check an online store before paying

A padlock icon is not proof that an online store is trustworthy. HTTPS encrypts the connection; it does not verify the seller. Before paying, look for independent evidence and consider a payment method with a dispute process.

## 1. Inspect the exact domain
Read the full address, not just the logo. Look for swapped letters, unexpected subdomains and links delivered through unsolicited messages. Find the merchant's website through an independent source. A recently registered domain alone does not establish fraud.

## 2. Verify the seller outside its website
Look for an identifiable business, usable contact details, delivery terms and a return policy. Search the business name alongside “complaint,” “scam” and “reviews.” Testimonials hosted by the seller are not independent verification. Where a business registration is claimed, check an appropriate official registry.

## 3. Examine the payment request
Be cautious when a seller insists on irreversible transfers, cryptocurrency or payments to a personal account. A card or payment service with a dispute procedure may offer recourse, but recovery is never guaranteed. Avoid entering payment information after following a suspicious message link.

## 4. Watch for pressure tactics
Unusually deep discounts, perpetual countdown timers and urgent “last item” warnings warrant extra scrutiny. No single indicator proves fraud; consider the pattern.

## 5. If you already paid
Preserve the order page, receipt and correspondence. Contact the seller in writing. If fraud is suspected, promptly contact your bank about securing the card and disputing the transaction. Change any exposed password through the official website and enable multi-factor authentication. Report suspected fraud to the relevant authority.

## Pre-payment checklist
- The domain matches the merchant you intended to visit.
- Contact and return information can be independently checked.
- Independent evidence reveals no serious contradictions.
- You understand your payment dispute options.
- You did not arrive through a suspicious payment link.

**Bottom line:** no checklist guarantees safety. Multiple warning signs are a good reason not to proceed.
