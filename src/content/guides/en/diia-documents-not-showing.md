## Diia documents not showing: safe troubleshooting

If a document disappears from the Diia app, that does not necessarily mean the underlying document is invalid. Display can depend on app availability, registry access and the accuracy of source data.

## 1. Determine the scope
Check Diia's official service notices. Is one document missing, are all documents missing, or is the entire app unavailable? Social-media rumors alone are not reliable outage confirmation.

## 2. Try safe basic checks
Confirm connectivity and that the app came from an official app store. Check for updates and reopen the documents section. Before signing out or reinstalling, make sure you can securely authenticate again.

## 3. If only one document is missing
Do not enter personal information into third-party sites promising to “restore” documents. Consult official guidance for that document type: data may come from a separate state registry. Where the underlying record needs correction, follow the responsible authority's official process.

## 4. If you urgently need identification
Ask the receiving institution which alternative document it accepts. Do not assume a screenshot is a legally equivalent substitute. Requirements depend on the document and context.

## 5. Contact support safely
Describe the document type, approximate time of failure, app version and error message. Do not publicly share passport details, tax identifiers or one-time codes.

## Quick decision path
- Everything fails: check official notices and connectivity.
- One document missing: review eligibility and the source registry.
- Document needed now: confirm an accepted alternative with the institution.
- Problem persists: use official support.

Verify service-specific instructions against diia.gov.ua and current official help pages before publication.
